# Dynamic Pricing in Ride-Sharing | Business Analytics Project

## About This Project
This project looks at how dynamic pricing works in ride-sharing platforms. It studies how demand, supply, peak hours, trip distance, and market conditions cause fares to go up and down.

## What I Did
- Analyzed data to find the key factors that affect ride prices and customer demand.
- Cleaned, analyzed, and visualized data using Excel, SQL, and Power BI.
- Built dashboards and insights to show demand patterns, peak pricing periods, and possible revenue opportunities.
- Studied the balance between customer affordability, driver availability, and platform profitability.
- Turned the findings into practical business recommendations to improve pricing strategies and day-to-day decisions.

## Tools Used
- Excel
- SQL
- Power BI

## Key Topics Covered
- Demand and supply
- Peak hours and peak pricing
- Trip distance
- Market conditions
- Customer affordability and driver availability
- Platform profitability

## Goal
To understand why ride fares change and to suggest better pricing strategies using data.
